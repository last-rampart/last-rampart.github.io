Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Publisher: LastRampart, independent publisher of the game Last Rampart (Android app com.lastrampart.game). Contact: last.rampart.game@gmail.com.

Last Rampart is a free single-player game, funded by advertising. Your game runs entirely on your device; only the ads, provided by Google AdMob, use the Internet. This page explains what is stored, what is sent, to whom, and how to control it.

What the game stores on your device

The game stores the following on your device, and we receive none of it:

  • your current campaign and current mission, so you can resume them;
  • unlocked companies, achievements and the collection of enemies you have met;
  • the recording of your last finished mission, so you can watch it again;
  • your settings (volume, language) and your ad consent choices;
  • reports of the game's latest errors (time, screen, device model, game version), which you can copy or clear from the settings.

If Android's automatic backup is enabled on your phone, Android may keep a copy in your Google account, according to your settings and under Google's responsibility, to restore it on a new device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. To select, show and measure these ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK built into the game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, from which Google may infer an approximate location (country, city), never your precise position;
  • technical information about the device and app: model, Android version, language, game version;
  • ad interaction data: impressions, clicks, ads watched to the end; and diagnostic data from the SDK itself.

We do not see this data individually: we only receive aggregated reports from Google (impressions, revenue). It is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" and in Google's Privacy Policy. Google may work with certified ad partners, listed in AdMob.

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent on first launch, through Google's consent form (certified under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). You can accept personalized ads, refuse them, or choose purpose by purpose. If you refuse, the game shows non-personalized ads, chosen from context (for example language or country) rather than from your profile.

You can change your mind at any time from the game's settings ("Ad privacy"). Where the law provides for it, including in some US states, the same button lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for advertising.

Controlling the advertising identifier

In Android's settings (Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads depending on the phone), you can reset or delete your advertising identifier. Once deleted, it is no longer sent to any app, and ads are no longer personalized.

If you report an error to us

When the game hits an error, the "Write to us" button opens your mail app on a pre-filled email: our address, a subject and the error report, which you can read and edit. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself. If you do, we receive that report and your email address. They are used only to fix the error and, if needed, to reply to you; they are neither shared nor used for anything else, and we delete the email on request.

What the game does not do

  • No account to create.
  • No analytics or automatic crash reporting, apart from the AdMob SDK.
  • No sensitive permission requested (contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, camera).
  • We do not sell your data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game erases all its data from the device. A copy kept by Android backup can be deleted from your Google account's backup settings. Data collected by AdMob is retained by Google under its own rules; you can manage your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to its processing, and withdraw your consent at any time. For the data we hold (error emails), write to us at last.rampart.game@gmail.com. For data processed by Google, contact Google. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its update date.
