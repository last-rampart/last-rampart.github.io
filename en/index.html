Turn-based medieval tactics

How it plays

Attacks are announced

Every enemy shows one turn ahead the tile it will hit. Make it miss, or send it into another monster.

Defend the hamlets

Every hamlet destroyed takes a point off the Rampart, and the Rampart lasts the whole run. At zero, you start over.

No two missions alike

30 places and 9 bosses, and in each place the enemies, reinforcements, weather and objectives are drawn at random. An optional challenge makes every mission harder.

Relics without numbers

30 rules that change the run, like walking on water or pushing enemies two tiles. No +1, no drawback.

Ten stages to Blackrock

Between missions, credits pay for your soldiers' upgrades. A mission takes a few minutes, phone in landscape. Free, supported by ads.

Three companies

The Designated Volunteers

Push, burn, harass, shoot straight: four trades, one Rampart.

None of them volunteered. The sergeant pointed.

The Stone Wardens

Raise walls and crush the enemy against them: charge, powder, hurl.

They care more about their stones than their pay. That says it all.

The Arcane Circle

Their blows, our targets: swap, turn around, pull, blow away.

Nobody knows who hired them. Neither do they, to be honest.
