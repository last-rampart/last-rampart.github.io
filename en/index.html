Turn-based medieval tactics and wallops

How it works, roughly

The monsters warn you

Every ugly shows one turn ahead the tile it's about to whack. Shove it to the right spot and the wallop misses, or lands on its mate. Why do they warn you? It's magic, don't ask.

Hamlets burn

Every hamlet destroyed takes a point off the Rampart, and the Rampart has to last the whole run. At zero, back to the start with your regrets. Joy.

The maps keep changing

Every map is generated, all 9 bosses included, and the enemy can turn up from any side. Enemies, reinforcements, weather and objectives are drawn at random, so don't bother memorising anything. An optional challenge makes the mission harder, for those who haven't learned yet.

Relics bend the rules

30 trinkets for walking on water or pushing enemies two tiles, with no +1 and no drawback. Fail a relic challenge and you get a "bad luck" instead. Serves you right.

Ten stages to Blackrock

Between missions, credits pay for your soldiers' upgrades, and they need them. A mission takes a few minutes, phone in landscape. Free, paid for by ads: somebody has to cover the wages.

The closed beta, meanwhile

The game isn't out yet, but it's looking for Android testers. It's free, it takes two minutes, and the steps go in order. Google likes order.

  1. 1. Join the group

    Open the testers' group and click "Join group", with the same Google account as your phone. Otherwise Google won't recognise you.

  2. 2. Become a tester

    Open the test page, with that same account, and click "Become a tester". Yes, that's it.

  3. 3. Install the game

    Install it from its Google Play page. It only opens for testers: if it sulks, give it a few minutes, Google has its garrison delays too.

Two favours: keep the game installed for at least 14 days (Google counts testers, and every uninstall sends me back to square one), and tell me what's broken through the report form. Even "it sucks" is welcome. With details, ideally.
