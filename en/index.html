You are the last rampart.

A turn-based medieval fantasy tactics game in pixel art. The monsters announce their blows: push them, burn them, turn their attacks against them, and save the hamlets of the Marches.

Coming soon to Google Play

Every turn is a puzzle

See every blow coming

Every enemy attack is shown on the board one turn ahead. No luck involved: you know what's coming, and it's up to you to stop it.

Their blows, your weapons

Push an orc into the water, move a wolf into an archer's line of fire, blow up a sapper among his own. The best move is often the enemy's.

A campaign to replay

Ten stages, from the outskirts to Black Rock. Over thirty maps and nine bosses, relics and upgrades. Every run is different; if your squad falls, you start again.

Made for your phone

Missions of a few minutes, playable one-handed in landscape. Three difficulties, and a challenge for those who want more.

Three companies

The Designated Volunteers

Push, burn, harass, shoot straight: four trades, one Rampart.

The Stone Wardens

Raise walls and crush the enemy against them: charge, powder, hurl.

The Arcane Circle

Their blows, our targets: swap, turn around, pull, blow away.
