Turn-based medieval tactics

How it plays

Attacks are shown in advance

When your turn starts, every enemy has already marked the tile it will hit. You can see who gets hurt and what breaks. Luck has almost no say.

You redirect the blows

A shield bash shoves the wolf into the path of the arrow meant for the hamlet. The troll ends up in the pond (trolls can't swim). The goblin sapper goes off in the middle of his friends.

The Rampart lasts the whole run

Every hamlet destroyed takes a point off it, and repairs are expensive. If it falls, you start over, with new maps drawn at random.

Ten stages to Blackrock

Over 30 maps and 9 bosses. Between missions, credits pay for upgrades and relics pile up along the way. A mission takes a few minutes, phone in landscape.

Three companies

The Stone Wardens

The Arcane Circle

Their blows, our targets: swap, turn around, pull, blow away.

Nobody knows who hired them. Neither do they, to be honest.
